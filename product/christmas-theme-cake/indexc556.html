          Description

          A cake full of festive cheer! This Christmas Theme Cake captures the holiday spirit with traditional colors, edible snowflakes, and classic Christmas elements. Whether for a family gathering or a holiday celebration, this cake is made with rich flavors that spread warmth and joy.

          Product Details:

          • Type: Cream Cake with strawberry designer cake
          • Eggless Cake
          • Weight: 1kg
          • Shape: Round |
          • Flavour: Black Forest | Serves: 7-9 people

          NOTE – “WE CAN MAKE THIS DESIGN IN ALL FLAVOURS”

          Care Instructions:

          • Store cream cakes in the refrigerator.
          • Fondant cakes should be kept in a cool, air-conditioned space.
          • Serve at room temperature, avoiding heat exposure.
          • Use a serrated knife for cutting fondant cakes.
          • Decorative elements may have toothpicks or wires—check before serving.
          • Consume within 24 hours for best taste.
